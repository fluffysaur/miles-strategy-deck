UOB Preferred Platinum Visa

Dual-cap 4.0 MPD powerhouse for mobile contactless & selected online spend

Milelion Review 4.0 MPD (2x $600 Caps)
Earn Rate: 4.0 MPD (UNI$10 / S$5)
Monthly Cap: S$600 Tap + S$600 Online
Rounding: S$5.00 blocks
Annual Fee: S$196.20 (First yr free)
Expiry: 2 Years
Transfer Fee: S$27.25 (Pooled with Lady's)

Dual Cap & Online Whitelist

Two separate S$600 calendar month caps: Mobile Contactless (in-store Apple/Google Pay) and Selected Online (Dining MCC 5811/5812/5814, HeyMax vouchers MCC 5311, Cinemas, Fashion).

SimplyGo Public Transport

SimplyGo bus/train taps are aggregated at month-end and rounded to the nearest $5 block (counts toward the contactless S$600 cap).

Physical Card Ban

Must tap phone via Apple/Google Pay. Swiping or tapping the physical plastic card earns 0.4 MPD only.

SMART$ Merchants

Avoid SMART$ stores (Giant, Cold Storage, Guardian) — earns SMART$ rebate instead of UNI$. Use Citi Rewards for these instead.
